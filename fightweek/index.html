What counts as a weekend?

Friday through Sunday in your timezone. “This weekend” includes the current weekend on Saturday or Sunday. “Next weekend” is the one after that. In the CLI, use --tz America/Denver to pick a timezone or --utc for UTC. No announced events? We’ll say so.
