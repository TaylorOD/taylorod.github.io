# scene scale and contact review

This revision keeps the original Missoula scenes and gives scenery, people and exports one 64 color palette without removing half the pixels. The same renderer produces the moving scene, contact sheets and static images.

The adult model keeps a 20 unit head, 21 unit torso and 19 unit legs. Its 60 unit standing height represents a 175 cm design reference. This is not a measurement of Taylor. Shorter fictional adults use 160 and 165 cm references. All 44 catalog entries now describe their physical reference, including animals, companions and people using equipment.

The arcade rear scale is 1.8 and the foreground reaches 2.1. Every person uses that same depth rule. Cabinet hips meet the stool at y 232, including shorter residents. The bartender stays below the long counter and left of its short return. The customer stands outside on the right. Their hands meet the counter.

The poker tabletop and pinball decks are raised to their interaction heights. The roof has a taller front guard with open gaps between posts. The house chair seat, keyboard, bookshelf and Scout contacts have explicit anchors. Wider house collision clearance keeps the standing body out of the couch. Sitting and standing blend over 320 ms instead of snapping between two positions.

Town hill depth changes continuously toward the house. Woodland animals use a separate distant plane. The height sheet projects them back to the street plane for comparison. Scout retains that plane through the chase and returns smoothly. Cyclists and shorter neighbors no longer use oversized or child sized adult silhouettes.

All 22 object comparisons fit their documented reference tolerances. Three former flags needed corrected references: the shop measurement included its transom, the couch is a low back sofa rather than the unrelated Eames product, and the roof bench uses a 50 cm park bench design. Those are reference corrections, not furniture resizing. The Wilma entry describes the illustrated six level building and is not a surveyed height of the real Wilma. Estimated image anchors remain labeled provisional in the ledger.
