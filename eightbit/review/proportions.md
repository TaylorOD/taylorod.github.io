# proportion review

Open `proportions.html` for body comparisons, scene overlays, the object ledger and every interaction destination. `scale.html` compares all 44 models on the same ground line. `models.html` shows their declared actions and facings.

The shared adult is a 175 cm design reference. This is not Taylor's measured height. The body stays 60 units tall with a 20 unit head, 21 unit torso and 19 unit legs. Previously the torso was 17 and the legs were 23. The head, shoulders and floor remain in place. Sitting and bending derive from the same torso proportions.

`scale-rules.js` owns body proportions, scene scale and campus depth. Characters and measurements use those same rules. A physical height projects vertically from an object's own floor anchor. Do not measure diagonally across a couch, desk or table. Do not scale a person separately to fit one object.

`scale-landmarks.js` records each object's target height, visible top, floor anchor, reference source, uncertainty and tolerance. Manufacturer dimensions are comparison objects, not claims about the furniture depicted in the painting. Unsourced dimensions are chosen design targets. The Wilma height is explicitly unverified.

The first ledger has 22 objects. Eight disagree with their targets, 11 have estimated anchors within tolerance, two have visible anchors within tolerance and one remains unmeasured. These results describe the current painting. They are not visual approval or proof of real world dimensions. The shop opening, distant house door, rear cabinet, stool, poker table, couch, roof bench and front rail need another art or anchor review. The distant house also needs its own depth treatment.

For each scene revision, check the marked floor anchors first, then physical dimensions, then the player and resident poses. Keep the body's proportions fixed. Review hands, hips, knees, feet and foreground masks in motion. Update the ledger only when an actual object measurement, chosen target or floor anchor changes. Keep estimated anchors provisional until the painting makes them clear.

All interaction arrivals have path clearance checks. Hand contacts use a 24 unit shoulder to hand limit. Tests also cover fixed hand targets, planted walking feet and model rendering. Those checks cannot approve silhouettes or furniture occlusion. The character registry leaves nonhuman physical targets and shorter human stature decisions open instead of assigning invented real world heights.

The backgrounds still have more texture than the sprites. This change corrects body proportions and makes scale disagreements reviewable. It does not claim the entire art system is finished.
