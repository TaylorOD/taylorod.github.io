# proportion review

Open `proportions.html` for body comparisons, scene overlays, the object ledger and every interaction destination. `scale.html` compares all 44 models on the same ground line. `models.html` shows their declared actions and facings.

The shared adult is a 175 cm design reference. This is not Taylor's measured height. The body stays 60 units tall with a 20 unit head, 21 unit torso and 19 unit legs. Previously the torso was 17 and the legs were 23. The head, shoulders and floor remain in place. Sitting and bending derive from the same torso proportions.

`scale-rules.js` owns body proportions, scene scale and campus depth. Characters and measurements use those same rules. A physical height projects vertically from an object's own floor anchor. Do not measure diagonally across a couch, desk or table. Do not scale a person separately to fit one object.

`scale-landmarks.js` records each object's target height, visible top, floor anchor, reference source, uncertainty and tolerance. Manufacturer dimensions are comparison objects, not claims about the furniture depicted in the painting. Unsourced dimensions are chosen design targets. The illustrated Wilma uses a six level, 16 m design target. This is not a survey of the real building.

The ledger has 22 objects and no remaining mismatches against the chosen references. Floor anchors hidden by furniture remain marked as estimated. The cabinet and stool now share a larger rear room scale. The house approach recedes toward its door. The poker table, pinball decks and front roof rail have corrected geometry. The shop door measurement excludes its transom. The sofa and park bench now use references for those furniture types rather than an unrelated sofa product and an indoor stool.

For each scene revision, check the marked floor anchors first, then physical dimensions, then the player and resident poses. Keep the body's proportions fixed. Review hands, hips, knees, feet and foreground masks in motion. Update the ledger only when an actual object measurement, chosen target or floor anchor changes. Keep estimated anchors provisional until the painting makes them clear.

All interaction arrivals have path clearance checks. Hand contacts use a 24 unit shoulder to hand limit. Tests also cover fixed hand targets, planted walking feet and model rendering. Those checks cannot approve silhouettes or furniture occlusion. The registry records design sizes for all 44 entries. The shorter adults are 160 and 165 cm. Scout and the squirrel share their scene depth, and woodland animals are projected to the street plane in the comparison sheet.

All scene layers and exported stills use one 64 color palette at the original world resolution. The contact sheet provides every destination at approach, arrival, contact and departure. Browser evidence and the completed correction list are in `scene-scale.md`.
