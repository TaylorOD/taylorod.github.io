# Flag

Included in 2.0. Follow the [shared game rules](README.md).

The [Connection manual](https://www.bandai.com/amfile/file/download/file/242/product/1304058/) has the pet raise flags to match arrows while ignoring imitation cues.

## Our version

Left and Right raise the matching flag. A Both button handles paired cues on touch. Keyboard players can use Space for Both. When the cue says Fake! Wait, leave all three buttons alone until the next cue. Waiting is the answer. Mark fake cues with a different shape, not color alone.

Play 15 cues. Get 12 right to win. Start with a 900 ms preparation beat, then allow 2.4 seconds per cue and a 900 ms rest between cues. Slower mode gives four seconds per cue. No repeated tapping for extra points.

## Screen and art

Put the cue above the display. Draw the pet holding lowered, left, right or both flags inside it. Keep the score and response timer below. Flags must fit beside the widest pets. Use the same full pet shown in the care screen.

## Check it

Test every cue type, fake cues, input at timeout, held keys and pause. One cue has one result. Do not require simultaneous touch presses. Include a slower mode and spoken cue labels before approving accessibility.
