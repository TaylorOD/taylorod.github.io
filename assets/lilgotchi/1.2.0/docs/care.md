# A little care goes a long way

Eggs hatch in 90 seconds. Then it’s time for food, play and sleep.

## The basics

| Control | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Feed | Meals for hunger. Snacks help happiness, but don’t overdo them |
| Play | A five round guessing game to cheer your pet up |
| Clean, Sleep or Wake | The third button changes with what your pet needs |
| More care | Medicine, discipline, lights and detailed stats |
| Settings | Help, Vacation, sound, backups and remembered pets |

Use medicine when your pet is sick. It may need more than one dose. Discipline is for unnecessary calls or misbehavior.

Turning the light off only darkens the screen. Sleep gives your pet a rest. The game also follows each character’s sleep schedule.

## Needs and growth

Food and happiness show how satisfied your pet is. Sleep restores energy. Health summarizes wellbeing, but a full health meter doesn’t mean a sick pet can skip medicine.

The care grade averages care over time. It starts after ten minutes and can be Perfect, Good, Normal or Bad. Care mistakes and discipline decide evolution, not the grade alone.

Each family stays its own kind of pet. See the [full family trees](characters.md).

## Taking a break

Turn on **Vacation** in Settings to pause care and growth until you return.

After an absence, the game catches up on at most eight hours. Closing just the drawer keeps care running while the page is open. It stays quiet.

## Keep your pet

Progress stays in this browser on this website. Clearing site data removes it. Use **Export save** in Settings to keep a backup, then **Import save** to restore it. Import shows the pet before you confirm replacement.

If another tab has your pet open, close that tab and choose **Try again**. If storage is blocked, progress may last only for the current visit.

Remembered pets keeps a small local history of pets that passed away. Exported saves contain the active pet, not that history.

## Keep a pet inactive

Pick the pet, open Settings and choose Make inactive. Its progress stays saved, but it stops growing and sending notifications. It stays in the selector. Choose Start pet when you want to return.
