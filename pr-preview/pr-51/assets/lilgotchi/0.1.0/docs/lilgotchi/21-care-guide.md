# Care guide

A four step guide lives in Save and help, outside the small game display. First visit welcome offers Quick tour or Got it. Dismissing either remembers the introduction preference. The guide remains available later from its disclosure.

## Steps

1. Feed, play and contextual care. Explain meals and snacks without suggesting unlimited snacks are safe.
2. Food, happiness, energy, advisory health and care grade. Explain the ten minute warmup and thresholds. Grades summarize wellbeing and do not replace original evolution rules.
3. Sleep versus light, medicine, discipline and growth. Explain fresh age zero adult presets.
4. Returning after an absence, the eight hour catch up limit, Vacation and local save backups.

## Interaction

Show one step at a time with Back and Next. The last step offers Done and Start again. Done collapses the guide and returns focus to its summary. Moving between steps focuses the heading and politely announces the step number. The guide never blocks gameplay or enables browser notifications. Every paragraph is plain text. No user content is interpreted as markup.

## Integration contract

`mountCareGuide(container)` appends one disclosure and returns its element, reset and destroy methods. Host CSS controls spacing and colors. The content assumes the eight hour away policy and Vacation controls are delivered in this release. Do not publish that copy without those runtime changes.

## Review

Check Quick tour, Back, Next, Done, replay and keyboard focus at phone widths. Verify the text against wellbeing thresholds and the final away settings. No duplicate tests for literal guide copy are added.
